House rules · 6 decks with jokers · 4–6 players

Hand Knee & Foot

Play in partnerships. Partners share books.

15Hand
13Knee
11Foot

♠The deal

Shuffle all six decks together. Everyone deals out their own cards: 15 for the hand, 13 for the knee, 11 for the foot. Keep the knee and foot face down and don’t look at them.

Start a discard pile with one card from the stock.

♥Your turn

  1. Draw 2 from the stock, or take the discard pile (below).
  2. Lay down cards: start a new pile with 3 or more of a rank, or add to your team’s piles.
  3. Discard 1 to end your turn.

Taking the discard pile: only if you hold 2 cards matching the top card and lay all three down right away. You take the entire pile.

♣Knee & foot

  • Knee: pick it up once your team completes its first book. You and your partner can both pick up at the same time. It goes into your hand.
  • Foot: pick it up once you have played every card in your hand.

♦Threes

  • Red 3s are banked right away: lay them face up in front of you as soon as you get them. On your first turn, draw a replacement for each red 3 you were dealt.
  • Each banked red 3 is worth 100. Seven together are worth 1,000, and each one after that adds 100. 5 red 3s = 500; 8 red 3s = 1,100.
  • Black 3s can’t go in a book. Get rid of them by discarding.
  • Any 3s still in your cards at the end count against you (see scoring).

♠Going out

You cannot go out until your team has all five required books. Then play out your foot to end the round.

♥Books

Seven cards make a book. Wild cards are 2s and jokers.

BookBonus
SevensSeven 7s, no wilds.5,000
FivesSeven 5s, no wilds.3,000
WildsSeven wild cards: 2s and jokers.2,500
CleanSeven of one rank, any suits, no wilds.500
DirtySeven of one rank with wilds mixed in, such as five 9s and two 2s. A dirty pile must always have more naturals than wilds, even while you build it: a pile of 6s needs more 6s than 2s and jokers.300

All five books above are required before anyone on the team can go out. The required sevens and fives must be clean. Once they’re closed, extra 7s or 5s piles may use wilds and score as dirty books.
